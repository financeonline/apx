## 1. Root causes (exact)

| Finding | Root cause in the code before ADR-124 (`8493362`) |
|---|---|
| **N-1** partly covered month carried | `run.ts` carried P−1's close into P whenever P−1 was "fully covered", whatever P's own coverage was; `cash.ts` made a month NULL only when the cash *basis* was `none`, and a GL (ledger basis) or a statement ending mid-month gave a basis; cash movements were always read from bank cells, so a ledger-basis month published a 0.00 movement and a carried close. |
| **N-2** stale typed opening | `cashCoveredBefore` counted a month as covered only if ONE statement spanned it, so a first month split across two statements made the NEXT month the chain's "first period", where `pickCandidate` accepted any typed balance dated on or before the period start — months earlier. |
| **N-3** joiner blanks history | A restart demanded an exact opening for EVERY covered account at once, and a month with any active account uncovered forced a restart; an account that joins later is uncovered before it joins, so every earlier month restarted and, lacking exact openings, went NULL. |
| **N-4** list outranks balance sheet | `invoiceSideCoverage` treated a list as fresh for P when committed on or after `P.period_start` — true of a list committed on 3 August for August, which knew nothing after the 3rd. |
| **N-5** mid-month journal double count | `ledgerFallback` resolved the journal at PERIOD granularity: it summed the journal's whole month, including the rows dated before the journal, which the journal's figures already contained. |
| **N-6** engine version not bumped | ADR-123 changed existing formulas and kept `2.0.0`; the sweep skipped any organisation that had EVER succeeded on the version, so figures a same-labelled earlier build wrote were never replaced. Nothing enforced the bump. |
| **N-7** 500 % share | `share = net overdue ÷ net total`; a credit note not yet due lowers the total and not the overdue part. |
| N-8 | `unreconciledIn` looked at every committed statement, including one a later upload superseded. |
| N-9 | A typed balance dated `P.start` was accepted at a chain start but not at a restart (`dayBefore` only). |
| N-10 | ADR-123's overlap clause dropped aged-debtors lists; the approved window alone would have let a list as at a later date state an earlier month-end it could not know. |
| N-11 | The overflow alert was dated in the run's LAST period, which can be a future month. |
| N-12 | NULL cash figures kept the basis's assurance (`verified`) and a coverage-derived completeness. |
| P-2 | Coverage by a single spanning statement: 5th-to-4th statements never span a month. |

## 2. What was built

### D-1 · Canonical per-account balance histories (`domain/cash-history.ts`, new)

- **Coverage** per account = the union of its committed statements' declared ranges (`coveredRuns`): statements that meet or overlap combine; a gap day breaks a run.
- **Anchors** = balances something states at the close of a day: a statement's printed opening (close of the day before its first day), its printed closing (close of its last day), the typed balance (close of `opening_balance_date`). Same day: printed before typed; latest committed statement first. A statement contained by a later-committed statement of the same account is not current (`currentStatements`) and states nothing.
- **Chain**: within a covered run `[s, e]` the balance starts at the earliest anchor dated in `[s−1, e]` and moves by the account's daily rows (`api_server.bank_daily_movements`, every row, transfers included). Later anchors in the run are cross-checks (`statement_balance_mismatch`; the arithmetic stands, §3.3). Nothing crosses an uncovered day. Outside a run a balance is known only on an anchor's exact day.
- **Per period**: required days = `P ∩ [start, end]`; movement known iff every required day is covered; opening = balance at the close of the day before the first required day; closing = opening + rows when both are known. An unknown closing never carries.
- **Organisation figures** (`computeCash`, rewritten to aggregate): `opening_cash` / `closing_cash` = Σ in-scope accounts, published only when all are known; `cash_inflows` / `cash_outflows` / `net_cash_movement` only when every in-scope account's movement is known (else NULL `no_source_covers_period` + `bank_coverage_partial`). Cash is bank data only — a ledger or balance-sheet basis never lends a month a cash figure. `closing_cash.components.per_account` records each account's balance.
- **No carried figures**: the history is rebuilt from the statements per chunk at the chunk's aggregation instant; `closing_cash` is no longer read back from `metric_current`. The org-level chain, `cashCoveredBefore`, `cashCoveredIn`, the restart logic, `openingCarry`, `openingExactOnly`, `statementOpenings`, `OpeningCashCandidate`, `pickCandidate` and `openingFor` are removed.
- The bank coverage test in `computeCoverage` (§2.2, `is_complete`) uses the same scope and union coverage (`bankCoverageFor`), so the completeness flag and the cash figures agree.
- Handled explicitly: account creation (scope start), activation/closure (`is_active`; a closed account ends at its last statement), first statement, multiple statements, gaps, overlaps, restart after a gap (an anchor at the boundary), missing opening, partial-month coverage, full-month coverage, account addition mid-month (joins with its balance the day before it starts).

### D-2 · Scope and naming

An account joins on the first day anything is recorded for it (first statement's first day, or the day after its typed balance). An active account about which nothing is stated is in scope everywhere and makes cash unknown; the NULL figures carry `components.missing_bank_accounts: [{account_id, name}]`, and the Cash page shows **"Your cash balance is not complete yet — Add a statement or an opening balance for …"** from that record (`loadCash().missingAccounts`). A closed account follows the lifecycle: counted up to its last statement, excluded if never stated.

### D-3 · Invoice-list freshness (`coverage.ts invoiceSideCoverage`)

Replaces ADR-123's rule exactly as approved. A batch of a side covers P when its declared range spans P, or:
- **completed P**: committed after `P.period_end`, ≤ 45 days after, and it states the position AT `P.period_end` — a `full` list whose range reaches it, or an `open_items` list whose as-at date (range end) is exactly `P.period_end`;
- **current P** (contains the run's as-of): committed on or after `P.period_start`, range overlapping P (unchanged).

One consistent application of the approved text recorded here: "an open-items list dated after P.end cannot state P" is applied to the spanning branch too — an aged-debtors list whose declared range spans a completed month but whose as-at date is later does not cover it (payments in between are not on it). A full list spanning P still covers it (it reconstructs any day inside its range). Invoice precedence over balance sheet and ledger is unchanged; a month the lists cannot state falls through to them.

### D-4 · Opening journals from their date

`loadOpeningJournal` reads `entry_date`; `ledgerFallback` picks the latest journal effective by `P.period_end` and sums from its DATE: for a journal dated inside a month, the run loads that month's lines dated before it (`loadLinesBeforeJournal`, opening lines included, so an earlier same-month journal is also removed) and subtracts them. The descriptor gains the optional filter `entry_date_from` (schema, compiler → `entry_date >= …`, `FILTER_ORDER`, row builder `gte`), emitted only for a journal dated after its month's first day, so the drill-down selects exactly the rows the figure summed.

### D-5 · Overdue share (`alerts/domain/rules/working-capital.ts`, templates)

`share = overdue open invoices ÷ all open invoices` of the side at the figure's as-of (`invoiceOutstandingAt`), credit notes summed separately as `open_credits`. The summary adds `overdue_invoices`, `open_invoices`, `open_credits`; the net figures (`overdue`, `total`) are untouched. Without the invoice population a net share above 100 % is withheld. Thresholds (30 % / 50 %, 10 days) unchanged. Sentence: "£1,000 of the £1,000 owed to you on open invoices is overdue (100.0%). Open credit notes of £800 bring what you are owed to £200." An alert stored before ADR-124 keeps its original sentence.

### D-6 · Engine versioning

- `ENGINE_VERSION = '3.0.0'` (`domain/version.ts`), carried onto every `calculation_runs` row; every snapshot, health score and alert references its run, so every persisted result identifies its engine version.
- **Sweep** (`api_server.enqueue_engine_upgrade`, migration `20260930000003`): an organisation is queued when its LATEST succeeded run is on a different version (was: when it had never succeeded on the version).
- **Stale results are not presented as current**: Home's "Updating your numbers" banner shows while the latest succeeded run's version differs from the deployed engine (`loadFiguresEngineVersion`), as well as while a job runs.
- **Recalculation** on the new version, whatever triggers it (sweep, manual, import), recomputes the whole history (`loadLatestEngineVersion`, unchanged) and — new — no cash figure depends on anything stored.
- **Enforcement**: `test/unit/engine-version-golden.test.ts` runs the pure engine over a fixed corpus covering every family (P&L on a ledger, per-account cash with 5th-to-4th statements and a joiner, invoices both sides, a balance sheet, a mid-month opening journal, the freshness matrix, the variance pass), hashes the canonical output, and compares it with `test/fixtures/engine-golden.json` `{engineVersion, hash}`: outputs changed with the version unchanged → FAIL; version changed → FAIL until recorded (`ENGINE_GOLDEN_RECORD=1`), and recording refuses to overwrite a hash for the same version.

## 7. Security and tenancy

- **RLS / server authority unchanged.** No table, policy or grant changed. The one new function, `api_server.bank_daily_movements`, is `security definer`, `search_path = ''`, owned by `motion_definer`, refuses callers without the service-role claim (42501), and is executable only by `service_role`; its body `app.bank_daily_movements` is revoked from public/anon/authenticated (pgTAP). The calculation worker calls it with the organisation id of the job it is running, as it calls `aggregate_periods`.
- **Recalculation cannot cross organisations.** Every new engine read (`bank_daily_movements`, `loadLinesBeforeJournal`, `loadStatements`) is filtered by the job's organisation id; the history is built per run for that organisation only. `enqueue_engine_upgrade` still queues through `app.enqueue_recalculation(org, …)`, one job per organisation.
- **Evidence cannot cross tenants.** `entry_date_from` is one more whitelisted column predicate (`entry_date >= …`) on a selection that still runs through the caller's RLS client with the as-of lifecycle filter. Proven in `adr124-remediation`: another organisation's owner and an unassigned employee resolve nothing; an assigned employee at aal2 resolves exactly 5,000.
- **Roles.** OWNER, ADMIN and CLIENT read their organisation's figures; an EMPLOYEE only an assigned organisation and only at aal2; a SUPER ADMIN only at aal2 — `receivables-recovery` M/N (unchanged, passing), `home-figures` "what each role can see" (passing), plus the new tenancy cases above.
- **Stale-figure banner** reads `calculation_runs.engine_version` through the member's own client under existing RLS; it discloses nothing beyond the version string already shown on the evidence page.
- **Fixtures cannot leak**: every fixture organisation is created through `api.create_organization` under its own owner, and each suite deletes its organisations and users by tag.
